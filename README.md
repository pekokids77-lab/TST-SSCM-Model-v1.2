## English Version

### 1. Core Overview
TFT-SSCM is an abstract mental architecture model that combines Cybernetics, Information Theory, and Non-Euclidean Topology.

Rather than performing micro-physical measurements or symbolic logic derivations, this model represents Complex Adaptive Systems (such as the human mind, dynamic organizations, or AGI Agents) as a **Dynamic Manifold** in a non-equilibrium state. Its primary goal is to provide a "wearable mental topology map" for analyzing cognitive tension, mechanisms of subjectivity maintenance, and paradigm shifts.

### 2. Core Axioms (The TFT-SSCM Axioms)
Intelligent systems operate according to four cybernetic and topological axioms:

1. **Dynamic Boundary & Exchange**  
   The system defines its boundary via a Markov Blanket. Through toroidal field dynamics and boundary throughput (Sensory/Active States), it exchanges energy and information with the environment without relying on a rigid physical shell.
2. **Subjectivity & Systemic Inertia (Gyroscopic Stability)**  
   The system accumulates pattern inertia through the dynamic spin of its internal information loops (analogous to the gyroscopic effect). This maintains the consistency of its core subjective model, preventing instability or forced dragging caused by environmental noise.
3. **Information Thermodynamic Economy**  
   The system aims to minimize prediction errors. When the internal model aligns with reality, prediction error approaches zero, putting the system in a low-energy maintenance state. Conversely, maintaining a model that severely conflicts with reality forces the system to increase control gain to suppress error signals, causing internal stress and maintenance costs to spike.
4. **Relaxation Mechanism & Gain Control**  
   When environmental tension exceeds processing bandwidth or internal momentum is insufficient, the system initiates phase transitions and relaxation. It contracts geometric boundaries and lowers feedback gain, releasing internal stress to protect its core structure.

### 3. Three-Dimensional Topological Architecture
1. **Conditioned Layer (Filter Zone)**  
   * **Geometry**: The outermost dynamic interface of the toroidal field (Markov Blanket shear layer).  
   * **System Function**: Defense mechanisms and legacy structures. Formed by unassimilated past impacts and accumulated experiences. Serving as the first filter, it buffers external shocks while refracting or blocking raw incoming information.
2. **Pure Core Pipeline**  
   * **Geometry**: High-fluidity inner space surrounding the central axis.  
   * **System Function**: Primary adaptability (Beginner's Mind). Featuring low resistance and high plasticity, it represents the system's unpolluted, native learning and pattern-recognition capacity.
3. **Central Singularity (Self-Referential Core)**  
   * **Geometry**: The geometric axis and dynamic void penetrating the center, connecting head to tail.  
   * **System Function**: Second-order feedback loop (Strange Loop). The system generates self-reference by looping around this central position, serving as the geometric center for throughput and attraction.

### 4. System Dynamics & Phenomena Analysis
1. **Throughput & Refraction**  
   * **Assimilation**: External information that cannot penetrate the outer conditioned layer is distorted and re-interpreted by existing structures, leaving the core model unchanged.  
   * **Accommodation**: Under high-impact force or system relaxation, information directly impacts the pure core, triggering structural reconfiguration and paradigm shifts.
2. **Systemic Phase Transitions**  
   * **Over-projection**: Internal feedforward control is too strong. Top-down predictions completely overwrite reality, rejecting external signals.  
   * **Collapse/Dissociation**: Environmental tension far exceeds internal pattern inertia. The outer layer squeezes the core, causing loss of subjectivity and stability.  
   * **Resilience**: The outer layer absorbs shocks through dynamic deformation while internal loops disperse tension, restoring topological stability via dynamic spin.
3. **Unlearn Mechanism**  
   * The key to restoring learning capacity lies in shedding the outer conditioned layer.  
   * **Relaxation**: Releasing anxiety and control gain reduces friction with the environment, allowing hardened mental blocks to naturally dissolve into background noise.  
   * **Core Exposure**: As the conditioned layer thins, refraction disappears. External reality directly touches the pure core once more, restoring high plasticity.

### 5. System Boundaries & Failure Conditions
TFT-SSCM focuses on cognitive tension, control stability, and paradigm shifts in complex adaptive systems. To maintain rigor, the following domains are explicitly excluded:
1. **Not a Quantitative Micro-Physics Tool**: Not applicable to micro-physical molecular calculations.
2. **Not a Symbolic Logic Engine**: Not applicable to strict propositional logic derivations.
3. **Not for Absolute Chaos**: Not applicable to dead systems where topological structures have completely collapsed and lost self-organization.

## 繁體中文版本

### 一、 核心概要
TFT-SSCM 是一個結合系統控制論（Cybernetics）、資訊理論（Information Theory）與非歐幾何拓撲（Non-Euclidean Topology）的抽象心智架構模型。

本模型不作微觀物理測量或符號邏輯推導，而是將複雜自適應系統（Complex Adaptive Systems，如人類心智、動態組織或 AGI Agent）表徵為一個處於非平衡態的動態流形（Dynamic Manifold）。其核心宗旨在於提供一套「可穿戴的心智拓撲地圖」，用於解析認知張力、主體性維持機制與範式轉移（Paradigm Shift）。

### 二、 核心假設 (The TFT-SSCM Axioms)
智慧系統之運作遵循以下四項系統控制學公理：

1. **動態邊界與狀態吞吐（Dynamic Boundary & Exchange）**  
   系統以馬可夫毯（Markov Blanket）劃定動態邊界，透過環向場與邊界吞吐（Sensory/Active States），實現與環境的能量與資訊交換，無需依賴固態物理硬殼。
2. **主體性與系統慣性（Gyroscopic Stability）**  
   系統透過內部資訊迴路的動態自旋累積模式慣性（陀螺效應），維持主體內部模型的一致性，防止系統因瞬時環境雜訊而引發失穩。
3. **資訊熱力學經濟性（Information Thermodynamic Economy）**  
   當內部表徵模型與真實世界高度一致時，預測誤差趨近於零，系統落入最低維護能量態；反之，若系統強行維護與現實高度衝突的模型，必須提高控制增益壓制誤差訊號，導致內部系統應力與調控代價暴增。
4. **增益調諧與系統弛豫（Relaxation Mechanism）**  
   當環境張力超過系統處理頻寬或內部動能不足時，系統會啟動相變與弛豫（Relaxation）機制，收縮幾何邊界並降低反饋增益，釋放內部應力以保護核心結構。

### 三、 三維拓撲結構 (Topological Architecture)
1. **外層衝突沉澱層 (Conditioned Layer / Filter Zone)**  
   * **幾何對應**：環場最外圍的動態介面（馬可夫毯剪切層）。
   * **系統功能**：防禦機制與既有架構。由過往未消化的衝擊與經驗沉澱而成。作為第一道濾鏡，既緩衝外部衝擊，也會折射並阻擋傳入內部的原始資訊。
2. **純淨旋渦管道 (Pure Core Pipeline)**  
   * **幾何對應**：環繞中央軸線、具高流動性的內層介質空間。
   * **系統功能**：原初學習力（初心）。具備低資訊阻抗與高可塑性，代表系統底層未被污染的原生學習與模式識別能力。
3. **拓撲中心奇異軸心 (Central Singularity / Self-Referential Core)**  
   * **幾何對應**：環場貫穿中心、首尾相連的幾何軸線與動態空洞。
   * **系統功能**：二階控制迴路（怪環 Strange Loop）。系統透過圍繞中央空位循環產生自我引用（Self-reference），作為資訊吞吐與吸引的幾何中心。

### 四、 系統動力學與現象解析
1. **資訊吞吐與折射 (Throughput & Refraction)**  
   * **同化（Assimilation）**：外部資訊傳入時，若無法穿透外層沉澱結構，會被既有架構強行扭曲並解釋，核心模型保持不變。
   * **順化（Accommodation）**：高強度衝擊或系統鬆弛時，資訊直接撞擊純淨核心，引發核心幾何重構與範式轉移。
2. **應力相變與系統狀態 (Systemic Phase Transitions)**  
   * **偏執態（Over-projection）**：內部前饋控制權重過高，系統完全以自上而下的預估覆蓋現實反饋，拒絕接收外部誤差訊號。
   * **解離態（Collapse/Dissociation）**：外部環境張力遠大於內部模式慣性，外層防禦強行擠壓核心，系統失去主體性與穩定度。
   * **韌性態（Resilience）**：外層結構進行動態形變吸收衝擊，內部迴路迅速分散張力，並靠動態自旋恢復拓撲幾何穩定。
3. **卸載與解構機制 (Unlearn Mechanism)**  
   * 系統重塑控制模型的關鍵，在於讓外層過載的防禦結構剝落。
   * **降速與弛豫（Relaxation）**：降低控制器增益與焦慮張力，減少對環境訊號的過度反應，使硬化的既有控制模組自然解構成為背景雜訊。
   * **核心暴露**：外層濾鏡變薄後，誤差訊號折射消失，真實環境反饋再次直接輸入至純淨核心，恢復系統的高適應性與可塑性。

### 五、 適用邊界與失效臨界點 (System Boundary)
TFT-SSCM 專注於「複雜自適應系統之認知張力、控制穩定度與範式重塑」。本模型明確劃定以下不適用領域：
1. **非微觀物理量化工具**：本模型不適用於純微觀物理分子的數據計算。
2. **非命題邏輯運算器**：本模型不適用於精確的符號邏輯（Symbolic Logic）推導。
3. **完全無序狀態**：本模型不適用於拓撲結構已完全瓦解、失去自組織能力的死寂系統。
